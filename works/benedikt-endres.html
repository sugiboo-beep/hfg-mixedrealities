The Floor is Lava

The Ground beneathA game of Perspective and future

Benedikt EndresDialog- und Regiefassung

A group of players enters a game. So far so good. But this game is not what they expect. As they are strangers to each other and all originally planned to play something different, they suddenly land in an exit room, as part of that very random group they are in now.

Whatever higher entity controls that game or the exit room as whole, it is only clear that its intentions are rather malicious. To win and come back into reality, the group has to form a team and play games that are frighteningly real, solve tasks they never solved before, and most importantly: survive.

Thereby the group begins to question rules and authority, determines whether we can influence certain things in life, finds strategies for handling crises and finds a safe space and community.

“The Ground beneath” is a performance that is being created as part of the seminar “The Floor is Lava” at the HfG Karlsruhe and the associated excursion to the Stromboli volcano. There is also a connection to Issue 4 (title: “The Floor is Lava”) of the HfG's online journal “Umbau”, in which the first scene of the performance will be published.
